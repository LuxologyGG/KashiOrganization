The firm

A boutique builder,
not a production line.

Founded in 2006 by Arash Abbasian, Kashi Organization focuses on new construction and whole-home builds across the South Bay, working closely with each client from start to finish to ensure they love the home they end up in. We deliberately stay small so the principal stays involved on every project, every week.

Over the years we've delivered tens of millions of dollars of work with an almost flawless track record: on time, on budget, and finished to a level of detail our clients and trade partners consistently call "second to none."
